Our Story
✦

Born from a love of tradition

Sakura Kaiten was founded in 1998 by chef Hiroshi Tanaka, who brought the kaiten-zushi belt concept from Osaka to Jakarta. Every piece we serve honours decades of Japanese culinary tradition — fish flown in twice weekly from trusted Tsukiji partners.

  • Fish sourced twice weekly from Tsukiji partner markets
  • Sushi rice seasoned with 30-year-aged Kyoto rice vinegar
  • Belt plates colour-coded by price — total transparency
  • Private omakase counter for up to 8 guests nightly
